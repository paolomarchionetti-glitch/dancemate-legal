Report content or behaviour

Open to anyone — no account and no app needed

If someone is in immediate danger, call 112 (the European emergency number) or your local emergency number.
This page is not an emergency channel: we read reports as soon as we can, but the mailbox is not staffed around the clock.

Use this page to report content or behaviour you believe is illegal or against DanceMate’s rules. This is the channel required by Article 16 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 on digital services, and it is open to everyone: no app, no account, no registration needed.

Do you have the app and can you sign in?
Reporting from the app is faster and reaches us already linked to the right profile or ad: open the profile or the ad, tap ⋮ and choose Report. This page is for people who can’t do that.

What to tell us

The more precise the report, the sooner we can act. Please include:

  1. Who or what you are reporting — display name, email address or profile link. If you don’t know, describe the person or the ad as best you can.
  2. What happened — when, where and how. One concrete fact is worth more than an opinion.
  3. Why you believe it is illegal or against the rules.
  4. Evidence you can show — just describe it (screenshots, messages, witnesses). Don’t attach files to the first message: if we need them, we’ll ask.
  5. Your email address — we need it to confirm we received the report and to tell you the outcome. We never show it to the person reported.

By sending the report you declare that what you describe is true to the best of your knowledge. Reports made in bad faith can themselves lead to measures.

What happens next

  1. We reply confirming that we received your report.
  2. We assess it. If needed, we ask you for a few more details.
  3. A person decides: automated tools may help us find content, but they don’t decide for us.
  4. We tell you the decision at the address you wrote from, together with the reasons.
  5. If you disagree, you can reply to that same email asking for a review.
Were you the one reported?
If you received a notice or a restriction and want to challenge it, you have 30 days from the decision: reply to the email we sent you, or write to us from here quoting the case code shown in the notice. If you can no longer sign in, give us the email address of your account. We look at it again and, if we got it wrong, we undo the measure.
We use what you send us only to assess the report and reply to you. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
