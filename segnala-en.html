This page is not an emergency channel: we read reports as soon as we can, but the inbox is not monitored 24 hours a day.

Here you can report content or behaviour you believe is illegal or against DanceMate's Terms. This is the notice mechanism provided for in Article 16 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 on digital services (“Digital Services Act”), and it is open to everyone: you do not need the app or an account.

Do you already have the app and can sign in?
Reporting from the app is quicker and reaches us already linked to the right profile or listing: open the profile or listing, tap ⋮ and choose Report. This page is for people who don't have that option.

What to tell us

The more precise the report, the sooner we can act. The Digital Services Act asks that a notice contain these elements:

  1. Where the content is — display name and city of the profile, title of the listing, and anything else that helps find it without doubt. If you have a screenshot, describe what it shows.
  2. What happened — when, where and how. A concrete fact is worth more than a judgement.
  3. Why you believe it is illegal or against the rules — if you have a specific law in mind, quote it; otherwise explain in your own words.
  4. Your name and email address — we need them to acknowledge receipt and tell you our decision. We do not reveal them to the reported person.
  5. A statement of good faith — that what you write is, to the best of your knowledge, accurate and complete. It is already included in the pre-filled message.

For content involving child sexual abuse you can report without giving your name and email. In that case also report it to the police (in Italy, the Polizia Postale). Never send us a copy of those images: describe them only.

Do not attach files to the first message: if we need them, we will ask you. Reports made in bad faith, or manifestly unfounded and repeated, may lead to us no longer processing reports from the sender for a period (Article 23 DSA).

What happens next

  1. We confirm that we received your report.
  2. We assess it diligently and objectively. If needed, we ask you for more details.
  3. A person decides: automated tools can help us find content, but they don't decide for us.
  4. We send you our decision, with reasons, at the address you wrote from.
  5. If you disagree, you can reply to that same email within 30 days asking for a review, free of charge, which a person will carry out again.
  6. You can also turn to an out-of-court dispute settlement body certified under Article 21 DSA, lodge a complaint with AGCOM, the Italian Digital Services Coordinator (agcom.it), or go to court.
Were you the one reported?
If you received a notice or a restriction and want to challenge it, you have 30 days from the decision: reply to the email we sent you, or write to us quoting the case code you find in the notice. If you can no longer sign in to the app, give the email address of your account. The review is done by a person and, if we were wrong, we cancel the measure.

Authorities and copyright

Judicial and administrative authorities can send orders and requests for information (Articles 9 and 10 DSA) to the same address, which is our single point of contact (Article 11 DSA), in Italian or English. Anyone who believes content infringes their copyright, image rights or trademark can use this same page, stating the right infringed and showing that they hold it or act on behalf of the holder.

We only use the data you send us to assess the report, reply to you and, where the law requires, inform the authorities. We keep it for 24 months from the closing of the case. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
