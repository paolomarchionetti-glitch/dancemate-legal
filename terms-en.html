1. What DanceMate is

DanceMate helps people find dance partners near them. You create a profile, publish ads, look for other dancers on the map or by distance, send an “interest” to someone who looks right, and if they accept, you exchange contact details.

DanceMate connects people: it doesn’t organise classes, doesn’t select partners and doesn’t guarantee any meeting. Contact details are exchanged only when the person who receives the interest accepts it and chooses which channels to share.

7. Points and rewards

  • Reports that turn out to be well founded earn points. How many depends on the seriousness of what you reported; repeated reports on the same case are worth progressively less, and there is a monthly cap.
  • Points are redeemed for days of Premium: 100 points for 24 hours, 250 for 3 days, 500 for 7 days. The current values are always shown in the App.
  • Points have no monetary value, cannot be transferred and are not refundable.
  • During a launch promotion points still accrue but cannot be redeemed: Premium is already open to everyone.
  • If a review overturns the decision on a report, the points earned for it are taken back.
  • Reporting in bad faith to collect points is a breach of these Terms.

8. Reports, moderation and sanctions

How to report

From the App, by opening a profile or an ad and choosing “Report”. Anyone without the App, or who cannot sign in, can use the public reporting page: that is the channel required by Article 16 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (DSA).

How we decide

  • Text is filtered automatically before publication; photos may be held for review. An automated service may suggest that an image is inappropriate, but a person always makes the decision.
  • Reports about the same subject are grouped into a single case and assessed together.

What can happen

Depending on seriousness and on what has happened before: a warning, content removal, a temporary restriction of some features, suspension of the account for a set period, up to exclusion from the service. The most serious breaches can lead straight to the strongest measures.

Reasons and review

  • Every measure comes with a statement of reasons, which you find in the App under “Account status” and receive as a notification.
  • You can ask for a review within 14 days of the decision, by writing to the address given in the notice and quoting the case code. We look at it again and, if we got it wrong, we undo the measure and restore what was taken away.
  • You remain free to turn to the competent authorities or to the out-of-court dispute settlement bodies provided for by the DSA.

9. Contacts and meeting outside the App

DanceMate makes contact easier, but it does not verify users’ identity — the “verified” badge only means a profile meets certain completeness requirements, not that anyone’s identity has been checked — and is not responsible for what happens outside the App.

Use common sense: meet in public places the first few times, don’t share details that aren’t needed, and report bad behaviour straight away. If someone is in immediate danger, call 112 (the European emergency number) or your local emergency number.
