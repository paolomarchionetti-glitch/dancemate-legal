2. What DanceMate is

DanceMate helps adults find dance partners in their area. You create a profile, publish listings, look for other dancers on the map or by distance, send an “interest” to someone who seems a good match and, if it is accepted, you exchange contact details.

DanceMate puts people in touch: it does not organise lessons or events, does not select partners, is not a party to any arrangement you make with each other, and does not guarantee any meeting. Contact details are exchanged only when the person receiving the interest accepts it and chooses which channels to share.

DanceMate is a leisure service. It is not a romantic or sexual dating service, and it may not be used to offer or seek sexual services or escort services.
