How it works

  1. Create your profile: your styles with your level, what you're looking for, a photo and, if you like, videos of you dancing.
  2. Search or post: filter people near you, or write a listing and wait for replies.
  3. Connect: send an interest; if it's accepted, you see the contacts the other person chose to share with you.

Questions

When is it coming out?

On Android by the end of 2026; on iPhone a few months later. If you sign up, we'll let you know on the phone you chose.

How much does it cost?

At launch it's free for everyone, with every feature. Later there will be an optional subscription for advanced features: you'll always be able to use the app for free, and what you create stays yours.

Is it a dating app?

No. It's made for dancers looking for a partner, a social-night companion, a course, students or teachers. Community rules forbid requests of any other kind, and every report is read by a person.

Who sees my number?

Nobody, unless you want them to. Contacts are only visible after you accept an interest, and you choose which ones to show (WhatsApp, Instagram, email…). You can revoke them any time.

Does it work outside Italy?

Yes: you can search and appear in any city. We're starting with the schools and social nights of Pesaro, Rimini and Bologna, but the app is for everyone.

I run a dance school: can I use it?

Of course: create your school's profile, post listings to find students or partners for your courses, and show your school's website to whoever you like.
