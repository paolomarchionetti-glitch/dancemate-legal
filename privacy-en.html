2. What we collect

We collect only what the dance-partner search needs to work.

Account

  • Email address and sign-in credentials, handled by Firebase Authentication. If you sign in with Google we receive the email and name of your Google account. The controller never has access to your password.
  • Display name.
  • Date of birth, used to check that you are an adult and to show your age to other users.
  • Gender.
  • When you accepted the Terms and this Policy, and which version you accepted.

Temporary or “disposable” email addresses are refused at sign-up: we compare the domain of your address against a list of known providers. Only the domain is compared.

Profile (you fill it in, and it is optional)

  • Your city, province and region.
  • Profile photo and gallery photos.
  • Bio, dance styles with their level, your goal and what you are looking for.
  • Height.
  • Links to public videos on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok. We store the link and a label: the videos stay on their platform, we neither copy nor host them.
  • Your contact details: WhatsApp, Instagram, contact email, website. They are stored separately from the rest of the profile and are not public (section 5).

Ads and interactions

  • The ads you publish: title, description, style and level, city, photos.
  • The interests you send and receive, with the optional introduction message, and their outcome (pending, accepted, ignored, cancelled).
  • The ratings you give and receive. They are stars only, from 1 to 5: there is no written comment. You can change or remove your rating.
  • The likes you give to a profile or a video, and the profiles you visit.
  • The people you block and the reports you send.
  • Profile verification requests, when you send one.

Technical and usage data

  • Last activity: when you last opened the App, shown to other users in approximate form.
  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging): a technical device identifier kept so we can send you notifications. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings; the token is removed when you delete your account.
  • Crash diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics): technical information about App errors, so we can fix them.
  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): off by default. They are collected only if you turn them on in Settings → Usage statistics, and you can turn them off again at any time.
  • An anti-abuse check (Firebase App Check) verifies that requests come from the genuine App and not from software imitating it.
What we do NOT collect. We never ask for or store a phone number (the WhatsApp field is a contact detail you choose to write, and it stays private). We never store your precise location. We don’t ask for your weight. We don’t handle payment data: purchases go entirely through Google Play, which doesn’t share your card details with us. We show no advertising and give no data to advertisers.

4. Your (approximate) location

  • We use Android’s approximate location permission, only while the App is open and only if you allow it.
  • The coordinate is rounded to two decimals — about one kilometre — before being saved, and the server rounds it again as a safeguard. Your precise location is never stored: what we keep identifies an area, not an address.
  • It is used to work out distances in search and to place you on the map. On the map the marker is shifted further, so it never points at an exact spot.
  • Other users see an approximate distance and a blurred marker: your coordinates are never shared, with users or with third parties.
  • The city you type in your profile is a label and does not decide your position on the map.
  • If you refuse or withdraw the permission, your location is not updated and you don’t appear in distance searches or on the map; the rest of the App keeps working. You can also hide your location from your profile settings.
  • Location expires after 7 days: if you don’t open the App for a week you stop appearing in distance searches until you open it again.

5. What other users can see

  • When your profile is visible, other users see your name, age, photos, city, approximate distance, styles and level, goal, bio, height if you entered it, your average rating, any profile videos, your ads and when you were last active.
  • Your contact details are not public. You decide who can see them, with four settings: everyone, only people whose interest you accepted (the default), Premium users only, nobody. When you accept an interest you choose which channels to share, one by one, and you can revoke them at any time from Settings → Shared contacts. If you choose “everyone”, you still pick which channels become public: the others stay private.
  • Who visited your profile and who liked it are shown as a count to everyone and as a list to Premium users.
  • Photos may be reviewed before becoming visible (section 6). Once published they can be reached through a direct link: don’t upload images you don’t want to be visible.
  • People you block can no longer find you in search or on the map and cannot send you interests.

6. Moderation, reports and points

Content checks

  • The text you publish (ads, bio, interest messages) is checked against a list of forbidden words: if something is wrong the App tells you before publishing.
  • Photos can be held for review: until they are approved they show an “under review” label and other users can’t see them.
  • When the feature is enabled, an automated Google Cloud service (Vision SafeSearch) analyses the image and produces a suggestion (for example “adult content likely”). It is only a suggestion: a person always makes the decision to approve or reject.

Reports and sanctions

  • You can report a profile or an ad from the App, and anyone can do so without an account from the reporting page.
  • Reports about the same subject are grouped into a case, which a person reviews. We keep the text of the report, who sent it, the decision taken and when.
  • Possible decisions range from a warning to content removal, a temporary restriction of some features, suspension, and finally exclusion. Every decision comes with a reason, shown in the App under “Account status” and sent as a notification.
  • You can ask for a review within 30 days of the decision, by writing to the address given in the notice and quoting the case code. If we got it wrong, we undo the measure.
  • To contain bad-faith reports we keep a reporter reliability score (a number from 0 to 100, starting at 50, that moves as reports are confirmed or rejected). It only helps us prioritise moderation work: on its own it never produces an automated decision about your account, and other users never see it.

Points

Reports that turn out to be well founded earn points, which you can redeem for days of Premium. We keep the ledger of those movements (how many points, why, when). If a review overturns the decision, the points awarded for that report are taken back.

The database and the photos are hosted on servers in the European Union. Some providers (Google in particular) may process data outside the EU: where that happens, the transfer relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission and the other safeguards set out in the GDPR.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile, ads and interactions: as long as you keep your account.
  • When you delete your account from the App, we delete your profile, photos, ads, interests, ratings, shared contacts and notification tokens.
  • What remains, for as long as community safety and legal obligations require, is the minimum information about moderation decisions concerning you, and the administrator’s action log.
  • Usage statistics, if you enabled them, follow the retention set in Analytics (generally up to 14 months).
  • Email correspondence about reports and reviews is kept as long as it is needed to handle the case and to defend against any dispute.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and to receive a copy in a readable format.

  • You can delete your account yourself, from Settings: this removes your data and your photos.
  • You can correct almost everything from your profile, at any time.
  • For a copy of your data, for corrections you can’t make yourself, or to withdraw a consent, write to paolo.marchionetti@gmail.com.
  • Optional consents — location, notifications, statistics — can be withdrawn at any time from the App or your phone settings.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

11. Minimum age (18+)

DanceMate is for adults only. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up and access is refused to anyone under 18. If we learn of an account belonging to a minor, we remove it.

12. Security

Traffic to our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to data is governed by database-level permissions (Row Level Security): every user can read and change their own data, not other people’s. Contact details are stored separately and can only be reached through the mechanism described in section 5. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not accessible to the controller.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant we will say so in the App. The current version is always published at this address.
