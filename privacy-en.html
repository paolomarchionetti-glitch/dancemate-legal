2. Data we collect

We only collect the data needed to run the search for dance partners and to keep the community safe.

2.1 Account

  • Email address and login credentials, managed by Firebase Authentication. If you sign in with Google we receive the email and name of your Google account. The password is never accessible to the controller.
  • Display name.
  • Date of birth, used to check that you are an adult and to show your age (not the date) to other users.
  • Gender.
  • Date and time when you accepted the Terms and this Policy, and the version accepted.

Temporary or “disposable” email addresses are rejected at sign-up: we compare only the domain of your email with a list of known domains.

2.2 Profile (you fill it in, and it is optional)

  • City, province and region of reference.
  • Profile photo and gallery photos.
  • Bio, dance styles with their level, goal and what you are looking for.
  • Height.
  • Links to public videos on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok. We store the link and a label: the videos stay on their platform, we do not copy or host them.
  • Your contact details: WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, contact email, website. They are stored separately from the rest of the profile and are not public (section 6).
Do not enter “special category” data. The profile does not ask for, and does not need, information about health, sexual orientation, political opinions, religious beliefs or ethnic origin. If you choose to write it in your bio or listings, you make it visible to other users: please don't.

2.3 Listings and interactions

  • The listings you publish: title, description, style and level, city, photos, and how many times they were viewed.
  • The interests you send and receive, with any introduction message, and their outcome (pending, accepted, ignored, cancelled).
  • The contact channels you chose to share with each person, and any revocations.
  • The ratings you give and receive. They are stars only, from 1 to 5: there is no written comment. You can change or remove your rating.
  • The “likes” you give to a profile, listing or video, and your visits to a profile or listing.
  • The people you block and the reports you send.
  • Profile verification requests, when you send them.

2.4 Purchases

For Premium subscriptions and Boost credits we keep the transaction data that Google Play sends us: product, purchase and order identifiers, date, expiry, status (active, cancelled, refunded…), whether it is a test purchase, and an encrypted identifier linking the purchase to your account. We also keep the Boost credit ledger (bought, used, refunded). We never receive your card or payment method details (section 9).

2.5 Community safety

  • Reports you receive or send, moderation cases, notices, restrictions and sanctions concerning you, review requests and their outcome.
  • The Safety Points ledger (section 7).
  • A reliability score for people who report (section 7).

2.6 Technical and usage data

  • Last activity: the time you last opened the App. It is used to show recently active profiles first in results; it is not shown to other users.
  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging): a technical device identifier, kept so that we can send you notifications.
  • Crash diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics): technical information about App errors — device model, system and App version, error trace — to fix them.
  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): off by default. They are only turned on if you choose so under Settings → Usage statistics, and you can turn them off at any time.
  • App verification (Firebase App Check): checks that requests come from the genuine App and not from a program imitating it. It uses a device attestation provided by Google Play.
  • IP address: like any online service, the servers we contact receive it in order to reply. We do not store it in a user profile.

2.7 Data stored only on your phone

The App stores some preferences on the device — language, display choices, sign-in state — in order to work. They stay on the phone and are deleted when you uninstall the App. The App does not use cookies or advertising trackers. These web pages only store the language you chose in your browser.

2.8 Data we receive from others

If someone reports you, including through the public reporting page, we receive from that person their description of the facts concerning you. If someone sends you an interest or rates you, we receive that interaction. If you sign in with Google, we receive the email and name of your account from Google.

What we do NOT collect. We do not ask for or store your phone number (the WhatsApp field is a contact you choose to enter, and it stays private). We never store your precise location. We do not access your contacts, camera or microphone: you pick photos from your gallery. We do not process payment data. We show no advertising, do no commercial profiling and do not sell or give data to anyone.

4. Which data is required

Email, display name, date of birth, gender and acceptance of the Terms are required to create an account: without them we cannot provide the service. All other profile data is optional. Refusing location, notifications or statistics does not prevent you from using the App: you simply don't appear in distance searches, don't receive notifications, or don't help us improve the App.

5. Your location (approximate)

  • We use the Android approximate location permission, only while the App is open and only if you allow it. The App never asks for precise or background location.
  • The coordinate is rounded to two decimals, about one kilometre, before it is saved — and the server rounds it again for safety. What we keep identifies an area, not an address.
  • It is used to calculate distances in searches and to place you on the map. On the map the pin is shifted further, so it never marks an exact point: in your profile settings you choose whether to show it in your area, only at city level (larger shift), or to hide it.
  • Other users see an approximate distance and a blurred pin: your coordinates are not disclosed to other users or to third parties.
  • The city you write in your profile is just a label and does not determine your position on the map.
  • If you refuse or revoke the permission, your location is not updated and you don't appear in distance searches or on the map; the rest of the App keeps working. You can also hide your location from your profile settings.
  • Your location expires after 7 days: if you don't open the App for a week you stop appearing in distance searches until you open it again.

6. What other users see

  • When your profile is visible, other users see your name, age, photos, city, approximate distance, styles and level, goal, bio, height if given, average rating received, any profile videos, your listings and any Premium or “verified” badge.
  • Your contact details are not public. You decide who can see them, with four settings: everyone, only people whose interest you accepted (default), only Premium users, nobody. When you accept an interest you choose which channels to share — WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, email, website — one by one, and you can revoke them at any time under Settings → Shared contacts. If you choose “everyone”, you still pick which channels to make public: the others stay private.
  • Remember that once you have shared a contact detail, the person receiving it may have noted or used it before revocation: revoking hides it in the App, it does not remove it from the memory or phone of someone who has already seen it.
  • Who visited your profile and who liked it is shown as a number to all users and as a list to Premium users.
  • Photos may be reviewed before becoming visible (section 7). Once published they can be reached through their direct address: don't upload images you don't want to make visible.
  • Users you block no longer find you in search or on the map and cannot send you interests.

7. Moderation, reports and points

Content checks

  • The texts you publish (name, bio, listings, interest messages) are automatically compared with a list of forbidden words: if something is wrong, the App tells you before publishing and you can correct it.
  • Photos may be held for review: until approved they show a “under review” label and are not visible to others. The review is done by a person.
  • If we switch on automatic image analysis in the future (Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch), the service will only produce a suggestion — for example “likely adult content” — and the decision will remain with a person. Today this feature is off.

Reports and sanctions

  • You can report a profile or listing from the App, and anyone can do so without an account from the reporting page.
  • Reports about the same subject are grouped into a case, which a person examines. We keep the text of the report, who sent it, the decision taken and when.
  • We do not reveal to the reported person who reported them, unless an authority or the law requires it.
  • Possible decisions range from a warning to removal of content, temporary restriction of some features, suspension, up to exclusion. For every decision you receive a statement of reasons, in the App under “Account status” and as a notification.
  • You can request a review within 30 days of the decision, by writing to the address given in the notice and quoting the case code.
  • When we become aware of information giving rise to a suspicion of a criminal offence threatening the life or safety of a person, we inform the competent authorities, as required by Article 18 of the Digital Services Act.

Reporter reliability score

To limit unfounded reports we keep, for each reporter, a number from 0 to 100 that starts at 50 and moves according to how many reports are confirmed or rejected. It is only used to decide the order in which cases are examined: it never produces a decision on its own about your account and is not visible to other users.

Safety Points

Reports that turn out to be well-founded earn points, which you can redeem for days of Premium. We keep the ledger of movements (how many points, why, when). If a review overturns the decision, the points awarded for that report are withdrawn. Support may correct the balance in case of error: every correction appears in your ledger.

8. Automated decisions and ranking of results

No decision producing legal effects concerning you, or similarly significantly affecting you, is taken solely by automated means (Article 22 GDPR). In particular:

  • the forbidden-words filter blocks the publication of a text, not your account: you can rephrase, and if you think the block is wrong you can write to us;
  • sanctions, photo removals and decisions on reports are always taken by a person;
  • rejecting disposable email domains is an automated check on the domain only: if your address is permanent and is rejected by mistake, write to us.

The order of search results and listings is calculated by automated rules that use no sensitive data and do not profile you for commercial purposes. What counts: distance, ratings received (adjusted for their number), profile completeness and verification, videos, recency of activity and of the listing. Premium profiles and listings, and those with an active Boost, are shown more prominently: this is a paid advantage, and it is always marked with an icon.

13. Your rights and how to exercise them

Under Articles 15-22 GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and obtain a copy;
  • rectify it if inaccurate or incomplete;
  • erase it (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restrict its processing;
  • receive it in a structured, machine-readable format and transmit it to another controller (portability);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing (section 8).

In practice:

  • you can delete your account yourself, from Settings;
  • you can correct almost everything from your profile, at any time;
  • optional consents — location, notifications, statistics — are withdrawn from the App or phone settings;
  • for everything else — a full copy of your data, a correction you cannot make yourself, an objection — write to paolo.marchionetti@gmail.com from your account's email address.

We reply within one month of the request, extendable by two further months for complex cases (in which case we let you know within the first month). Exercising your rights is free. To protect your data we may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by replying from your account's email address.

14. Minimum age (18+)

DanceMate is for adults only. At sign-up we ask for your date of birth and no account is created for anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we close it and delete its data. If you are a parent and think your child has signed up, write to us.

15. Security and data breaches

Communications with our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to data is governed by database-level permissions (Row Level Security): each user can read and change their own data, not other people's, and sensitive operations go through controlled server functions. Contact details are stored separately and can only be reached through the mechanism described in section 6. Passwords are managed by the authentication provider and are not accessible to the controller. Administrative access is reserved to the controller and protected by a second credential.

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a personal data breach likely to pose a risk to your rights, we will notify the Italian Data Protection Authority within 72 hours and, if the risk is high, we will also inform you, as required by Articles 33 and 34 GDPR.
