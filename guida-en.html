1. Before you start

{{NOME_APP}} helps you find people to dance with: a competition partner, a companion for social nights, someone to take a course with, students or teachers. It works in three ways, which you can combine:

  • Listings: you write what you're looking for («Looking for a bachata partner for social nights in Rimini») and wait for replies.
  • Search: you look for people yourself, filtering by style, level, distance and much more.
  • Map: you see who dances and who is looking around you, or in another city.

Getting in touch is protected: first you send an interest; if the other person accepts, they decide which contacts to show you (WhatsApp, Instagram, email…). Nobody sees your number unless you want them to.

Requirements

  • You must be at least 18.
  • You need an Android smartphone (the iPhone version will come later).
  • The app is available in Italian and English; you can switch language any time.

Tap the photo and pick an image from your phone. Every new photo is checked before it's published: while it's under review you'll see a note saying it will be visible after approval.

A photo where your face is clearly visible, maybe while dancing, makes all the difference. Without a photo you can't see other people's contacts.

5.5 Dance styles and level

  1. Tap to add your first dance style (then add more).
  2. Choose from over 60 styles, in alphabetical order: from Bachata to Zouk, including Liscio, Argentine Tango, Standard and Latin, Swing, Hip Hop and many more. If yours isn't there, choose Other.
  3. For each style choose your level: Beginner · Basic · Intermediate · Advanced · Class D · Class C · Class B · Class A · Class S · Professional. The classes are the federal competition ones; if you don't compete, use Beginner to Advanced.
  4. Use the up/down arrows to set the order: the first ones appear first on your cards.
  • Free up to 3 styles · Premium unlimited.
  • If you have more than 3 styles and go back to Free, the first 3 stay visible: put your most important ones at the top.
Giulia has danced bachata for years, salsa for a short while, and wants to start tango. She enters: Bachata — Advanced, Salsa — Basic, Argentine Tango — Beginner. Anyone searching for «advanced bachata» finds her straight away, and a tango teacher knows she's just starting.

5.6 Videos Premium

  • Up to 5 videos, as a link from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok (other sites aren't accepted).
  • Each with a description of up to 40 characters (e.g. «Regional final 2025»), which you can edit.
  • Others open them with Open; you can take them down with Remove.
  • Search has a With video filter: a video makes you easier to choose.

6. Location: how it works and what others see

  • Your location comes from your phone, only if you grant permission, and only the approximate one (not precise GPS).
  • It's read when you open the app, rounded to about one kilometre and stored that way. Your precise location is never stored.
  • On the map others see your pin moved further, according to the precision you chose (5.10).
  • If you revoke the permission, your stored location is deleted and you stop appearing in distance searches and on the map.
  • The city on your profile is just a label: it doesn't change where you appear.
  • The badge on your profile tells you whether your location is up to date. If not, just reopen the app with the permission on.
Sara lives in Forlì but often dances in Rimini. Her profile says «Forlì»; when she opens the app in Rimini her location updates, and people in Rimini find her nearby. To look for partners in Bologna without going there, she uses Search from another city.

12. The map

12.1 What you see

  • Open it from the globe 🌍 in the bottom bar, or from the globe at the top of Home and Search: from there the map shows the same results, with the filters you have already chosen.
  • At the top choose what to show: Listings or Users.
  • Pins are coloured by gender: blue man, pink woman, purple other, grey-blue not specified. A smiley face is a person, a ♪ note is a listing (coloured by its author's gender).
  • When several pins are close together they become a purple circle with a number. Tap it: the map zooms in until they separate.
  • Tap a pin: a card appears at the bottom; tap it to open the profile or listing.

12.2 Moving around

  • Drag to move, pinch with two fingers to zoom. The map doesn't rotate.
  • My location (bottom right) takes you back to your saved location, even if you had chosen another city, and reloads.
  • Zoom follows the radius: with 25 km you see the surroundings, with All Italy you see Italy.

12.3 Map filters

Tap Filters: they're the same as Search (for users) or Home (for listings), with the same Free/Premium rules. Gender and age apply to people, not listings. With Search from another city Premium the map moves there, abroad too.

Anna is on holiday in Barcelona: in the map filters she picks «Barcelona — Catalonia, Spain» and sees the bachata listings in the area.

12.4 Privacy on the map

No pin is in the exact spot: the location is rounded and then moved according to each person's chosen precision (6). People who chose «Hidden» don't appear.

13. Interests: the first step

An interest is the way to say «I'd love to dance with you». There are no free-text messages: this protects everyone from pestering and harassment.

13.1 Sending an interest

  1. From a profile or a listing tap I'm interested.
  2. Choose one ready-made sentence from six themes (A hello · Let's dance · Same styles · Socials & events · Practice & growth · Let's connect), or no message.
  3. Send it: you'll see a confirmation.
  • Limit: Free 5 interests in 24 hours · Premium 30.
  • One interest per person: if you try again you'll be told you've already sent one, or that you're already connected.
  • You can withdraw it while it's pending: Cancel interest.

13.2 Where to find them

From your profile tap My contacts: two tabs, Received and Sent. Each interest has a status:

StatusMeaning
PendingThe other person hasn't replied yet.
Connected ✓They accepted: you can see the contacts you've shared with each other.
Not answeredThe request was ignored. It isn't said explicitly, out of respect for both.
Under reviewRare: the interest is being checked.

13.3 Replying to an interest you received

  • Accept: a share your contacts panel opens. Switch on the channels you want to show that person and press Accept and share, or Accept without sharing (you can share later).
  • Ignore: the interest goes away; the other person sees «Not answered».
  • At any time: Cancel connection ends the connection.
Marco sends Giulia the interest «Would you like to dance together at one of the next socials?». Giulia gets the notification «Marco is interested!», looks at his profile and videos, taps Accept and shares only Instagram. Marco gets «Giulia accepted! 🎉» and sees her Instagram, but not her WhatsApp.

16. Blocking and reporting

If you're in immediate danger call 112. {{NOME_APP}} is not an emergency service.

16.1 Blocking

From a profile (⋮ menu at the top) tap Block user. It's immediate and only affects you: that person can no longer find you in search or on the map and can't send you interests. They aren't notified. To unblock: Settings → Blocked users → Unblock.

16.2 Reporting

On a profile (⋮ menu → Report) or on a listing (flag). Reporting has the case reviewed by a member of our staff.

  1. Choose the reason:
    • Personal safety, stalking
    • Harassment or threats
    • Sexual content or requests
    • Underage user
    • Scam or fraud
    • Pretending to be someone else
    • Fake profile
    • Inappropriate content
    • Someone else's photos or data without consent
    • Collecting contacts / spam
    • Duplicate or off-topic listing (listings only)
    • Other
  2. For many reasons we ask Where did it happen?: Inside {{NOME_APP}} · During or after a meet-up · On WhatsApp · On Instagram · Somewhere else. What happens outside the app counts too: it helps us understand what we can verify.
  3. Write What happened?: it really helps. Dates, words, circumstances.
  4. Confirm the declaration («I declare that what I have reported is true to the best of my knowledge») and send.
  • The person you reported will never know it was you.
  • You can send up to 5 reports in 24 hours; the same person or listing can be reported once while the case is open.
  • If many of your reports turn out to be unfounded, your reliability drops and the limit becomes 2 reports in 24 hours.
  • If others have already reported the same thing, we tell you: reports end up in the same case.
  • Without an account you can report from the public Report page.
  • For technical problems or anything else: Settings → Report a problem or content.

16.3 Tips for meeting up

  • The first time, meet in a public place: a social night, a school, a rehearsal room.
  • Tell a friend.
  • The ✓ badge means we checked the profile, not that the person is trustworthy: always use common sense.
  • Nobody should ask you for money: if it happens, report it as «Scam or fraud».

17. After a report: measures and appeals

Every report is read by a person. If it's founded, the measure is proportionate. Whoever receives it is always notified, with reason and duration, in Settings → Account status and notices (unread notices are highlighted with NEW).

NoticeWhat it means
WarningFounded report, no restriction: a reminder to follow the rules.
Formal warningAs above, but if it happens again the account may be restricted or suspended.
Content removedA photo, text or listing was taken down. The account stays active.
Features temporarily restrictedUntil a date you can't publish or edit listings, send interests or leave ratings. Everything else works.
Account temporarily suspendedYou can't use the app until the date shown.
Account excludedPermanent exclusion, for serious violations.
Review in progressWhile we check a report your profile doesn't appear in searches; otherwise you use the app normally.
Review concludedThe check ended without measures.

17.1 Asking for an appeal

Every measure has a code. If you think we got it wrong, within 30 days write to the address shown in the notice, quoting the code. The case is reopened and, in the meantime, the measure is lifted. You'll receive one of these notices:

  • Appeal in progress — we're reviewing; you can use the app normally.
  • Appeal upheld — you were right: the measure is cancelled.
  • Appeal concluded — we confirm the decision.

18. Safety Points

When one of your reports is confirmed you earn points, which you can turn into Premium without paying. You'll find them in Settings → Safety Points.

  • How many: 10 points for a confirmed report, up to 20 if it was decisive, 3 if the case was already known thanks to others.
  • Rewards close together: if you've already received other rewards in the last 7 days, the new one is worth less. The second is worth 80%, the third 50%, the fourth 20%; from the fifth on you earn no points until the earlier rewards fall outside the 7-day window.
  • Cap: at most 100 points in 30 days. Once reached, later confirmed reports will pay again further on.
  • Reporting at random doesn't pay: unfounded reports lower the weight of yours.
  • If a case is reopened and cancelled, the related points are clawed back (you'll see it in your history).

23. Your data and your account

  • A copy of your data: Settings → Request my data prepares the request; we reply by email.
  • Deleting your account: Settings → Delete account, then confirm. Profile, photos, listings and contacts are deleted and this can't be undone. If you have a subscription, cancel it first from Google Play. If the phone asks you to sign in again for security, sign out, sign back in and try again.

24. Frequently asked questions

I don't appear in searches: why?

Check three things: that Visible profile is on (5.10), that your location is up to date (badge on your profile, 6), and that there's no Review in progress (Settings → Account status and notices).

I accepted an interest but the other person says they can't see my contacts.

You probably accepted without sharing, or you use «Only Premium users» and the other person is Free. Go to Settings → Shared contacts and check the channels for that person.

I can't publish a listing.

You need at least one contact on your profile (5.8). If you already have the maximum number of active listings (2 or 10), deactivate or delete one. If your account has restricted features, it's written in Account status and notices.

My listing is «Under review» or «Rejected».

Usually there's a contact (phone, email, link) or a disallowed word in the text. Remove it with Edit: if it was rejected, it's republished automatically.

My photo doesn't show.

Every new photo is approved before it's published: you'll see «under review» until it's checked.

I can't see anyone's contacts.

Add a photo and your city to your profile: they're required to see other people's contacts.

I've reached the interest limit.

On the free plan you can send 5 in 24 hours; with Premium 30. It counts the last 24 hours, not the calendar day.

Someone is bothering me outside the app, on WhatsApp.

Block them in the app and report them choosing «On WhatsApp» as the place. If you feel in danger call 112.

The map is empty.

Check the mode (Listings or Users), the radius and the active filters. If you're in an area with few members, try «All Italy» or another city.

What happens to my data if I delete my account?

It's deleted; some information related to reports and measures may be kept for the time stated in the Privacy Policy.

I changed phone: do I lose Premium?

No: sign in with the same account. If you have a subscription, tap «Restore purchases» in Premium.

Can I use the app in Italian?

Yes: Settings → App language, or the selector at the top of your profile. Notifications and legal pages follow the language you choose.
